Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How Excel Automotives collects, processes, and protects your personal data in compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

Excel Automotives operates from Units 4–7, 875 Sheffield Road, Sheepbridge, Chesterfield, S41 9EF. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information. You can reach our data contact by telephone at 01246 455 863.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, book an appointment, or have work carried out on your vehicle, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Full name, telephone numbers, email address, postal address.
  • Vehicle information: Registration number, make, model, VIN/chassis number, mileage, service history, and diagnostic logs.
  • Payment transaction records (we do not store full credit card numbers).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to fulfill our contractual commitments: executing vehicle repair orders, recording MOT test results on the DVSA portal, sending annual service/MOT reminders where requested, and maintaining statutory accounting records under UK tax laws.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with official regulatory entities (such as the DVSA for MOT testing), parts suppliers (for vehicle VIN verification), and secure digital accounting systems.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate records, or request erasure where data is no longer necessary for legal or warranty obligations.
