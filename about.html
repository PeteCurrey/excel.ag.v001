Independent Heritage

Built on reputation, sustained by repeat trust.

Excel Automotives was established over 30 years ago to provide motorists in Chesterfield, Dronfield, and South Yorkshire with a dependable alternative to franchised dealerships.

Led by Managing Director Dave Brown and our seasoned team of technicians, the garage expanded across Units 4 to 7 at 875 Sheffield Road. When Dave Brown incorporated Browns Autocare into Excel Automotives, it consolidated decades of mechanical knowledge, diagnostic capability, and customer loyalty under one continuous roof.

We have never operated on commission or aggressive sales targets. Our technicians are motor vehicle engineers who treat every vehicle — from a family hatchback to a cherished classic or modern SUV — with meticulous care.

The Excel Standard

How We Do Business Every Single Day

The principles that have kept Chesterfield drivers coming back for over 30 years.

No Work Without Approval

We never perform unauthorized work. If our inspection reveals an unexpected advisory or defect, we call you with clear options, costs, and honest urgency ratings before turning a single bolt.

Transparent, Fair Invoicing

Every invoice clearly breaks down genuine OEM or manufacturer-approved parts and skilled labour hours. No hidden shop fees or mysterious diagnostic surcharges.

Clean & Respectful Care

We respect your property. Seat covers, floor mats, and steering wheel protectors are standard protocol. Your car is returned clean, road-tested, and ready for miles ahead.

Manufacturer Warranty Safe

Under EU/UK Block Exemption Regulations, having your vehicle serviced at Excel Automotives using OE parts preserves your full manufacturer warranty, at a fraction of main dealer labour rates.

Workshop Capabilities

Equipped for modern motoring challenges.

Modern vehicles are sophisticated computers on wheels. That is why we continually invest in industry-leading diagnostic platforms, dedicated MOT testing lanes, 3D laser wheel alignment, and air conditioning recovery stations for both R134a and R1234yf refrigerants.

Specialist Workshop Equipment Includes:

  • DVSA-approved Class 4 and light commercial automated MOT testing bay
  • Dealer-grade OBD-II electronic diagnostic scanners and oscilloscope testing
  • Volvo-dedicated software tools, module coding, and VIDA-compatible diagnostics
  • Dual R134a and R1234yf climate control service and leak detection stations
  • Precision multi-camera laser tracking and high-speed dynamic wheel balancing
