Cookie Policy

How we use essential and analytical cookies to ensure our website functions quickly, securely, and reliably.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They help websites remember preferences and understand how visitors interact with online tools.

2. How We Use Cookies

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies (such as remembering form input steps during booking and security tokens) and privacy-preserving performance analytics to measure site traffic and popular service pages.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your browser settings to block or notify you about cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact interactive booking features such as the DVLA MOT lookup tool.
