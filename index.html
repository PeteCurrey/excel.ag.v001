About Excel Automotives

An independent garage that works for you.

We've been looking after Chesterfield's vehicles from our Sheepbridge workshop for over 30 years. We're not part of a national chain and we don't have sales targets — just a team of experienced mechanics who take genuine pride in honest work.

That means you get a straight answer on what your car needs, a fair price, and the work done properly. If something can wait, we'll tell you. If it's urgent, we'll explain why.

We service and repair every make and model — and we have a particular passion for Volvos, with dealer-level expertise at independent garage prices.
