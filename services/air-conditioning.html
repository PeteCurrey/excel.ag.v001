Car Air Conditioning Regas & Repairs in Chesterfield

Stay ice-cool in summer and demist rapidly in winter. Full diagnostic testing, vacuum leak checking, and re-gas with R134a & R1234yf refrigerants.

Complete Automotive Climate Control Servicing

A vehicle air conditioning system naturally loses around 10% to 15% of its refrigerant charge each year through rubber hoses and seals. Over time, this results in warm air from the vents, strained compressors, and increased fuel consumption.

At Excel Automotives, we operate automated recovery and recharging equipment capable of handling both older vehicles running R134a refrigerant and newer post-2017 vehicles requiring modern R1234yf gas.

What Our Air Con Service Includes:

  • Refrigerant Recovery & Weigh: Safe evacuation of residual gas and measurement of system loss.
  • Deep Vacuum & Moisture Extraction: 20-minute deep vacuum cycle to remove moisture that causes internal acidity and corrosion.
  • Nitrogen Pressure Leak Testing: High-pressure nitrogen hold test to confirm system integrity before introducing gas.
  • PAG Oil & UV Dye Replenishment: Adding fresh synthetic compressor lubricant and UV tracer dye for future leak visibility.
  • Exact Gram Weight Recharge: Recharging exact factory-specification refrigerant quantities according to manufacturer technical data.
  • Vent Temperature & Performance Check: Digital probe verification ensuring air vent output drops into the optimal 3°C – 7°C window.

Musty Smells Coming From Your Vents?

Bacteria, fungi, and mould can accumulate on the dark, damp surface of your vehicle's evaporator coil behind the dashboard. We provide an antibacterial sanitisation treatment and pollen filter replacement that eliminates odours and restores fresh, healthy air.
