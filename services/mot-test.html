Impartial, Professional MOT Inspections

Every vehicle in the UK aged 3 years or older requires an annual MOT test to verify roadworthiness, safety, and exhaust emissions under DVSA regulations.

At Excel Automotives, our purpose-built automated MOT bay features modern hydraulic shaker plates, roller brake testers, and connected laser headlamp alignment systems. Our certified testers examine your vehicle strictly according to DVSA guidelines — never inventing faults or exaggerating advisories.

What Is Inspected During Your MOT Test?

  • Braking Efficiency & Balance: Roller brake test assessing pad wear, disc condition, hydraulic lines and ABS/ESC functionality.
  • Tyres & Road Wheels: Tread depth (minimum 1.6mm legal requirement), tyre structure, sidewall cuts and wheel condition.
  • Steering & Suspension: Ball joints, track rod ends, shock absorbers, coil springs and power steering pumps.
  • Emissions & Exhaust: Tailpipe gas analyser check for petrol hydrocarbons/CO and diesel particulate opacity.
  • Visibility, Lights & Signals: Windscreen wipers, washers, glass condition, headlight beam alignment and indicator signals.
  • Body Structure & Safety Items: Seatbelts, airbags, horn, chassis corrosion and battery security.

What Happens If Your Car Fails?

Don't panic. If your vehicle fails its MOT, we provide a clear, itemised quote for the exact remedial work required. You are under no obligation to have the repairs done with us. If we complete the repairs within 10 working days, your MOT re-test is completely free.
