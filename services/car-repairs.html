Mechanical & Electrical Engineering

Car & Van Repairs in Chesterfield

From diagnostic fault-finding and brake overhauls to suspension replacements and welding, we fix problems accurately and affordably.

Accurate Diagnostics, Long-Lasting Repairs

When your car develops a strange noise, an engine warning light, or a handling issue, guessing parts can become an expensive headache. Our seasoned vehicle mechanics pinpoint the root cause before replacing components.

We service all makes and models including Ford, Vauxhall, Volkswagen, Audi, BMW, Skoda, Renault, Peugeot, Land Rover, Nissan, and Toyota.

Our Repair Disciplines Include:

  • Computer Fault Diagnostics & ECU: Advanced engine management, ABS, airbag (SRS), body control, and sensor live-data logging.
  • Braking Systems: Replacement brake discs, premium friction pads, callipers, master cylinders, brake hoses, and handbrake cables.
  • Suspension & Steering: Coil springs, shock absorbers, wishbones, anti-roll bar links, tie rods, and power steering racks.
  • Exhaust Systems & DPF: Complete exhaust replacement, catalytic converters, flexi-pipes, exhaust manifold gaskets, and particulate filters.
  • Cam Belts & Timing Chains: Preventive cambelt kit renewals with new water pumps and tensioners to prevent catastrophic engine failure.
  • Cooling & Heating: Radiators, water pumps, thermostats, heater matrix repairs, and head gasket replacements.
  • MOT Welding & Fabrication: Chassis welding, sill repairs, suspension mounting repairs completed to strict DVSA MOT pass standards.
