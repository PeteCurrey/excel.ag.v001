Precision Maintenance

Manufacturer-Schedule Car Servicing in Chesterfield

Keep your car running smoothly, prevent costly mechanical breakdowns, and safeguard your manufacturer warranty without dealer markups.

Complete Servicing Tiers for Every Mileage & Age

Routine servicing is the most cost-effective investment you can make in your vehicle. Regular fluid replacements, filter renewals, and thorough mechanical inspections preserve engine performance and fuel efficiency.

Interim Service

Every 6 Months / 6,000 Miles

Ideal for high-mileage drivers or urban commuter vehicles requiring regular health monitoring between full services.

  • Engine oil flush and premium manufacturer-spec synthetic oil replacement
  • OEM oil filter replacement and sump plug washer renewal
  • Fluid top-ups: brake fluid, coolant, windscreen washer, power steering
  • Visual brake check, tyre pressure / tread inspection, and steering play check
Most Popular

Full Service (Annual)

Every 12 Months / 12,000 Miles

Our comprehensive annual service covering all core mechanical and safety components across 60+ checkpoint procedures.

  • Everything included in the Interim Service
  • Engine air filter replacement and cabin pollen filter change
  • Full brake inspection (wheels removed, callipers, pads, discs and drums cleaned)
  • Suspension, shock absorbers, bushes, ball joints and CV gaiters inspection
  • Battery health check, alternator charging rate, starter motor test
  • Service light reset & digital service book stamped / updated

Major Service (Bi-Annual)

Every 24 Months / 24,000 Miles

The ultimate deep inspection replacing long-life consumables such as spark plugs, fuel filters and specialised transmission fluids.

  • Everything included in the Full Service
  • Spark plugs replacement (petrol) or diesel fuel filter replacement
  • Brake fluid boiling point test and full hydraulic system flush if required
  • Air conditioning operational check and coolant anti-freeze concentration test

Does Servicing at Excel Invalidate My Warranty?

No. Under UK Motor Vehicle Block Exemption Regulations, you have the legal right to have your vehicle serviced at any reputable independent garage without voiding your manufacturer warranty. We use original equipment (OE) matching quality parts and follow manufacturer schedules to the letter.
