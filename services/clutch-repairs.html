Complete Clutch & Dual Mass Flywheel Replacement

A worn or failing clutch can leave you stranded by the roadside. At Excel Automotives, we replace hundreds of clutches each year across all makes and models using premium clutches from OEM manufacturers including LuK, Sachs, and Valeo.

Common Signs Your Clutch Needs Attention:

  • Clutch Slipping: Engine revs increase under acceleration but the vehicle fails to gain speed accordingly.
  • Difficulty Engaging Gears: Grinding sounds when selecting reverse or stiff resistance engaging 1st gear when stationary.
  • High or Spongy Biting Point: The clutch only engages at the very top of the pedal travel, or the pedal feels soft and spongy.
  • Clutch Judder & Vibrations: Shuddering through the car floor or pedal when pulling away from a standstill, often pointing to a warped pressure plate or failing Dual Mass Flywheel (DMF).
  • Rattling / Squealing Noises: Strange noises that disappear when the clutch pedal is depressed, indicating a worn release bearing or concentric slave cylinder.
