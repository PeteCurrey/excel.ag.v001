Service Agreement

Terms & Conditions

Our standard terms of service for repair, maintenance, MOT testing, parts supply, and courtesy vehicles.

1. Authorisation of Work

No work is undertaken on any vehicle without prior customer authorisation. Where additional defects are discovered during diagnostic dismantling or servicing, we contact the customer with an itemised estimate and await explicit approval before proceeding.

2. Estimates and Quotations

Quotations are provided based on diagnostic visual inspection and manufacturer technical data. Unless otherwise stated, all prices include parts, skilled labour, and VAT where applicable.

3. Parts and Warranty

All replacement parts fitted by Excel Automotives meet or exceed original equipment manufacturer (OEM) specifications. New components carry standard manufacturer warranties (typically 12 months / 12,000 miles) against mechanical defect.

4. Loan Vehicles & Courtesy Cars

Courtesy cars are provided free of charge subject to availability and the driver holding a valid UK driving licence with appropriate insurance coverage. The vehicle must be returned with equivalent fuel and in clean condition.

5. Payment and Collection

Payment in full is required upon collection of the vehicle unless formal commercial account terms have been agreed in advance. Accepted payment methods include major debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and cash.
