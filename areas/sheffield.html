A61 Corridor Access

Garage Servicing & Volvo Specialists for Sheffield Drivers

Avoid main dealer overheads and city centre garage charges. Direct dual-carriageway access from South Sheffield straight to our Sheepbridge workshop.

A Trusted Dealer Alternative for South Sheffield

A significant proportion of our loyal customer base travels from Meadowhead, Woodseats, Millhouses, Norton, Dore, Totley, and Abbeydale. Thanks to the fast A61 Dronfield Bypass, reaching Excel Automotives takes barely 10 to 15 minutes from south Sheffield suburbs.

In particular, Sheffield Volvo owners choose us for dealer-level software diagnostics, expert timing belt renewals, and AWD Haldex servicing without paying franchise rates.

Why Sheffield Drivers Head South to Excel:

  • Substantial Savings vs Franchise Dealers: Equivalent diagnostic depth and OE parts at competitive independent labour rates.
  • Fast A61 Bypass Link: Quick, hassle-free drive without congested city-centre traffic or parking charges.
  • Free Loan Car: Drive back home or to work in Sheffield while your car is serviced on our ramps.
  • Direct Mechanic Communication: Speak directly with Dave Brown or the technician working on your engine.

Directions from Sheffield (Meadowhead Roundabout)

Take the A61 south towards Chesterfield along the Dronfield Bypass. At the end of the bypass (Whittington Moor / Sheepbridge junction), take the B6057 exit onto Sheffield Road. Excel Automotives is located 400 yards ahead at Units 4–7, 875 Sheffield Road.
