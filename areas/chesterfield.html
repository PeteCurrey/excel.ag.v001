Providing dealership-calibre MOT testing, mechanical repairs and servicing for Chesterfield families and business fleets for over three decades.

Serving Chesterfield Drivers from Sheffield Road

Located just off the A61 at Units 4–7, 875 Sheffield Road in Sheepbridge, Excel Automotives is ideally positioned for motorists throughout Chesterfield, including Whittington, Brimington, Hasland, Brampton, Walton, and Staveley.

Whether you need an annual MOT, a major service before a long journey, or quick assistance with an illuminated engine warning light, our technicians are ready with honest advice and transparent pricing.

Why Chesterfield Motorists Choose Excel:

  • Over 30 Years Local Heritage: Established independent presence with multi-generational customer trust.
  • Free Local Collection & Delivery: We can collect your vehicle from your home or workplace anywhere in Chesterfield.
  • Free Courtesy Loan Cars: Keep moving while we work on your vehicle (mention when booking).
  • Strict 'No Surprise' Policy: Zero unauthorised work — we call and agree every item in advance.

Visiting from Chesterfield Town Centre?

Simply head north on the A61 Sheffield Road for approximately 2.5 miles. Look for Units 4–7 at number 875 in Sheepbridge on your left-hand side, just before Dronfield.
