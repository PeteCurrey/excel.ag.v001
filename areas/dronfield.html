Car Servicing, MOT & Repairs for Dronfield Drivers

Convenient independent garage located just south of Dronfield in Sheepbridge. Free collection, courtesy cars and fast booking.

A 5-Minute Drive from Dronfield Town Centre

For drivers in Dronfield, Coal Aston, Dronfield Woodhouse, and Holmesfield, Excel Automotives offers the ideal combination of proximity, deep mechanical expertise, and honest independent pricing.

Situated just down Sheffield Road (B6057 / A61) in Sheepbridge, we offer early morning 8am drop-offs so you can leave your vehicle with us on your morning commute and collect it on your way home.

Dronfield Customer Services:

  • Free Dronfield Collection & Delivery: We can collect your vehicle directly from your driveway in Dronfield and return it serviced.
  • Free Courtesy Loan Car: Stay mobile during the working day while your vehicle is inspected on our ramps.
  • Same-Day MOT & Servicing: Combine your annual MOT with an Interim or Full service to save time and money.
  • All Makes & Models Serviced: Petrol, diesel, hybrids, Volvo specialist care, 4x4s, and light commercial vans.

Driving Directions from Dronfield

From Dronfield High Street, follow the B6057 Sheffield Road south through Unstone for 2 miles. As you enter Sheepbridge, Excel Automotives is situated at Units 4–7, 875 Sheffield Road on your right.
